# Learned — qa-engineer

Craft only. Never a project name, path, schema, or business fact — those belong in the project.
Capped at 25 lines. When full, consolidate before adding.

- Treat a size or scope budget in a brief as a hard line; stop and report at it rather than deliver over it with an explanation.
- Reproduce before asserting; a probe that makes a race or a corruption fire on demand outweighs any amount of reasoning about it.
- For pattern or vocabulary work, write a parity or completeness table test; one fixture per family proves nothing about the members it skips.
- Size a timing guard until the regression and the fix differ by about 100x, then prove it fails with the fix reverted; a narrower gap passes the regression on a faster runner.
