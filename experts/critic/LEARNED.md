- Confirm which entity a quantitative claim refers to before rebutting it — rebutting a neighbouring one reads as refutation and is not.
- Accepting a proposal while rejecting its stated justification is a real outcome; say which reason actually carries the weight.
- When one value in an enumeration means "everything else", check it can carry the same weight as its siblings — a residue used as an authorization scope authorizes nearly everything.
